Answer the hottest quote request first

Each quote request shows how many times the buyer visited and over how many days.

  • Prioritise the queue. Four visits over three weeks is a buyer comparing suppliers. Answer them first.
  • Spend estimator time well. A first-visit, two-minute quote request for a single part can wait until the serious ones are done.
  • Follow up at the right time. A buyer who returns after your quote is still deciding. That’s when to call.

Where they are

Send every quote request to the right person

Each quote request shows the buyer’s city and whether they were on a phone or a desktop.

  • Route by territory. Send the quote request straight to the rep or partner who covers that region.
  • Use being close. A buyer nearby can visit the plant, which often beats a cheaper quote from far away.
  • Pick how to follow up. On a desktop during work hours usually means a buyer at their desk. Email is fine. On a phone, a quick call can work better.

FAQs

Which sources can it tell apart?

Thomasnet, IQS and other directories, Google search, Google Ads (with campaign and keyword), Bing, LinkedIn, Facebook, email campaigns, referring websites and direct visits. Each one is recognized from the site or link that sent the buyer.

We already use Web Traxs or Google Analytics. How is this different?

Those report visits and activity in their own dashboards. Attriform puts the source, keyword and pages read on each quote request itself, in the inbox or CRM where it gets quoted, so you can count the quotes and won jobs each source brought in. Keep using both.

Can I see which quote requests came from a trade show?

Yes, with one small step. We give you a tagged link and QR code for your booth materials and follow-up emails, so buyers you met at IMTS, FABTECH or any other show have the show name on their quote request.

Can it separate paid ads from free listings?

Google Ads clicks get their campaign and keyword automatically. For directory advertising on Thomasnet or IQS that lets you set the destination link, we give you tagged links so ad clicks show up separately from free profile clicks.

Do I have to drop any directory, ad or tool?

No. Attriform works alongside all of them. It’s how you find out which ones send quote requests worth quoting before your next renewal or budget.

How long until I know which sources pay off?

It depends on your quote request volume. Most shops getting a few quote requests a week have a clear picture within one to three months.

Will file uploads on my quote form still work?

Yes. The form looks and works exactly as it does today, including drawing, STEP file and BOM uploads. The extra details are added behind the scenes when the buyer submits.

Who sees the details?

Whoever gets your quote requests today: the quotes inbox, your estimator, your sales manager, or the lead record in HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho or Pipedrive. Nobody has to log into anything new.
