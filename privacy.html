Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy covers attriform.com: what we collect when you visit, book a call or send us your details, why, and who we share it with. If you’re a client, the tracking we set up on your website is covered by your own privacy policy, not this one.

Attriform (“we”, “us”) provides lead source tracking setup for business websites. Questions about this policy or your information: ritwik@attriform.com.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, work email, company name and company website.
  • Call bookings: when you book through our calendar, Calendly collects your first and last name, email, phone number and anything else you enter. We receive these from Calendly.
  • Email: anything you send us at our email address.

Information collected automatically

  • Visit details: pages you view, the page or link that referred you, campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source), ad click IDs, how many times you’ve visited, your browser and device type, and your IP address and the approximate location it indicates (country, region, city, postcode).
  • Cookies and similar storage: see the table in section 4.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to you, run the call you booked and provide our service.
  • To see how you found us and which pages you read, so we can prepare for the conversation. This is the same kind of lead source information our service gives our clients.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including telling advertising platforms when someone books a call or sends the contact form, so they can measure results and show our ads to relevant people.
  • To keep the site secure and working, and to prevent spam.

We don’t sell your personal information for money.

5. How long we keep it

We keep contact form submissions, booking details and emails for as long as we’re in conversation or working together, and then only as long as we need them for records, legal or accounting reasons. Advertising platforms keep data under their own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ads and cookies: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings or with a content blocker, and change your ad settings in your Meta and Google account preferences.
  • Opt out of sharing for advertising: email us and we’ll stop sending your details to advertising platforms. We also honour the Global Privacy Control signal from browsers that send it.
  • Access, correction and deletion: depending on where you live (including California and other US states with privacy laws), you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and opt out of targeted advertising. Email ritwik@attriform.com. We won’t treat you differently for asking.

7. Security

We use reputable providers and HTTPS for every page and form, and limit who can access your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children

This site is for businesses and isn’t intended for children under 16. We don’t knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the site or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
